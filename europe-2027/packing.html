Avoid trouble

Don't pack · packing tips

Don't pack

  • Meat or milk products — you can't bring them into the EU or Switzerland from India. Biscuits are fine.
  • Liquids over 100 ml in the cabin bag — and it's all cabin bag this time.
  • Power banks in checked baggage — cabin only.
  • The Indian 3-pin socket strip — it won't fit Swiss or French sockets; one universal adapter is enough.
  • A big winter coat — two snow days don't need one. Layers do the job and fit in the bag.
  • Too much cash — cards work everywhere, even for a coffee.

Packing tips

  • Roll clothes into three packing cubes: tops, bottoms, inners and socks.
  • Wear the heaviest things on the plane — walking shoes, jeans, fleece, jacket.
  • Snow days: spare socks in the daypack, and a polythene bag for wet things.
  • Laundry in Interlaken on Tue 25 halves the clothes you carry.
  • Dorm kit: padlock, earplugs, eye mask, Crocs for the shower.
  • Weigh the backpack at home — aim for 7 kg or your airline's limit.
