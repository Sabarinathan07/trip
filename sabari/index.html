Avoid trouble

Don't pack · packing tips

Don't pack

  • Chewing gum — importing it into Singapore is banned. Take mints.
  • Vapes or e-cigarettes — illegal in Singapore.
  • Power banks in check-in — airlines refuse them.
  • Liquids over 100 ml in the cabin bag.
  • Too much cash — cards work almost everywhere; hawkers and farms want small notes.

Packing tips

  • Roll clothes and use packing cubes: one for tops, one for bottoms, one for inners and socks.
  • Wear the heaviest things on the flight — jeans, jacket and the Nike Air.
  • Keep a wet bag (polythene) for rafting and Universal Studios clothes.
  • Cash: about SGD 100–150 and RM 250–300 in small notes is enough.
  • Leave 8–10 kg free in the suitcase for shopping at Mustafa, Orchard Road and the Genting outlets.
  • Weigh the suitcase at home; 30 kg is the airline limit, so aim for 22–24 kg going out.
