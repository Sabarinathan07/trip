Your Visa perks, checked for these dates

Pelago · VISASG15

15% off Singapore activities, capped at SGD 15 per booking, new users only. Valid to 30 Nov 2026.

Play it: Have several family members open their own Pelago account and book 1–2 Universal Studios tickets each — every booking gets its own SGD 15 cap.

Sentosa Discovery Choice Pass

One complimentary Sentosa attraction worth up to SGD 55 per Visa card. Valid to 31 Mar 2027.

Play it: Redeem online in advance. Every adult with their own Visa card picks Wings of Time — the whole group sees the show free.

Dine & Save with Visa

Up to 20% off the bill at participating restaurants in Singapore and Malaysia. Valid to 31 Oct 2026.

Play it: Check dineandsave.visa.com/listing before booking each premium dinner; quote the offer when paying.

Din Tai Fung RM 20 off

Was Johor Bahru outlets only and ended 15 Sep 2026 — not usable in KL on this trip.

Play it: Din Tai Fung at Pavilion KL stays on the plan as a lunch option, just at full price.

FRI 16 OCT · CHENNAI → SINGAPORE

Arrive and settle in

  • 14:20 depart Chennai; land Changi 21:20 (+2.5 hrs time change)
  • Immigration and bags ~45 min — have Arrival Card QR codes ready
  • Pre-booked maxi-cab to your aunt's home (Grab can't guarantee 8 seats late)
  • Late supper if needed: Springleaf Prata or Al-Azhar, both open 24 hours
Tip · Download Grab and SimplyGo before landing; SIM or eSIM at the Changi arrival counters.

SAT 17 OCT · SINGAPORE

Marina Bay from morning to lights

  • 10:00 Merlion Park, walk the waterfront to Marina Bay Sands
  • 12:30 Lunch at Lau Pa Sat hawker centre (premium: Din Tai Fung, MBS)
  • 14:30 Gardens by the Bay — Flower Dome & Cloud Forest, 2–2.5 hrs indoors
  • 18:30 Dinner at Satay by the Bay, outdoors, easy for eight
  • 19:45 Garden Rhapsody at Supertree Grove, then Spectra at MBS (20:00 / 21:00 / 22:00)
Tip · Premium dinner swap: LAVO rooftop at MBS or Jumbo Seafood chilli crab at Riverfront.

SUN 18 OCT · SINGAPORE → CRUISE

Chinatown, then set sail

  • 09:30 Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Chinatown street market
  • 11:30 Early lunch at Maxwell Food Centre — Tian Tian chicken rice
  • 13:00 Taxi to Marina Bay Cruise Centre (15 min); arrive at the start of your check-in window
  • Onboard: reserve Dream Dining Room and Zodiac theatre seats on the app
  • Overnight sailing to Port Klang
Tip · Check your e-ticket for the exact boarding window — with eight people, be first in the queue.

MON 19 OCT · PORT KLANG → KUALA LUMPUR

Batu Caves and Jalan Alor

  • 08:00–09:00 Disembark; pre-booked 10-seater van waits at the terminal
  • 10:30 Batu Caves (1 hr 15 drive) — 272 steps, modest dress, sarongs for rent, zip your bags
  • 12:30 Banana-leaf lunch at Rani Vilas or Sri Ganapathy by the caves
  • 14:30 Check in to the KL apartment (Bukit Bintang / KLCC), rest
  • 18:30 Jalan Alor street food: Wong Ah Wah wings, Beh Brothers Hokkien mee, fruit stalls
Tip · Ask the Oct 21 van operator to quote a 3-day package covering the port pickup too.

TUE 20 OCT · KUALA LUMPUR

Petronas Towers, KLCC and shopping

  • 09:00 Petronas Twin Towers Skybridge + Level 86 deck (pre-booked morning slot)
  • 10:30 KLCC Park and the fountain lake
  • 12:00 Lunch at Suria KLCC — Madam Kwan's nasi lemak and cendol
  • 14:00 Pavilion KL (Din Tai Fung, Level 6) and Lot 10 Hutong food court
  • 16:30 Optional: The View at Merdeka 118, or Central Market for souvenirs
  • 19:30 Premium dinner with the towers lit: Marini's on 57, Fuego at Troika, or Nadodi
  • 21:00 Lake Symphony fountain show at KLCC
Tip · Check the Dine & Save listing — one of the three dinner picks may give 20% off.

Where to sleep in KL, Oct 19–20

Expect RM 900–1,500 per unit per night in mid-October. Multi-bedroom units sell out — book this week.

WED 21 OCT · KL → SINGAPORE

The long drive south

  • 08:00 Depart KL in the private 10-seater van (about 4.5 hrs to the border)
  • Lunch stop: Yong Peng (Fook Yuen Hakka food) or a Malacca Jonker Street detour (+1.5 hrs)
  • Border: everyone leaves the van for immigration on both sides — allow 1–2 hrs
  • Ask the driver for Tuas Second Link, far quicker for vans than Woodlands
  • 16:00–18:00 Arrive; easy dinner near your aunt's home
Tip · New Singapore Arrival Cards for all eight must be submitted before this crossing.

THU 22 OCT · SENTOSA

Universal Studios Singapore

  • Weekday hours 10:00–18:00 — arrive 09:30; MRT to HarbourFront, Sentosa Express to Waterfront
  • Ride order: Transformers → Battlestar Galactica → Revenge of the Mummy → Jurassic Park Rapids
  • Lunch inside the park; keep ponchos handy for the water rides
  • Dinner at Resorts World: Malaysian Food Street (casual) or Osia Steak & Seafood Grill (premium)
Tip · Book on Pelago with VISASG15 across several new accounts to stack the SGD 15 caps.

FRI 23 OCT · SENTOSA

Beaches, luge and Wings of Time

  • 10:00 Cable car from Mount Faber down to Siloso Beach
  • 11:00 Skyline Luge and Skyride (combo tickets on Pelago)
  • 13:00 Lunch at Coastes on the sand, or Ola Beach Club
  • 15:00 Singapore Oceanarium — indoors for the hot hours, ~2 hrs
  • 18:00 Dinner at Tanjong Beach Club (book) or Quayside Isle
  • 19:40 Wings of Time — free with each Visa Discovery Choice Pass
Tip · Redeem the Sentosa pass online before the trip; each adult's Visa card earns one ticket.

SAT 24 OCT · MANDAI

Singapore Zoo and Night Safari

  • 10:00 Singapore Zoo — Splash Safari and Elephant presentations; MRT to Khatib + Mandai shuttle, or two Grabs
  • 13:30 Lunch at Ah Meng Restaurant inside the zoo
  • 15:30 Rest, or River Wonders for the Amazon boat ride and giant pandas
  • 18:00 Dinner at Ulu Ulu Safari Restaurant
  • 19:15 Night Safari first tram — book early, Saturdays fill
Tip · Book the Night Safari tram slot today; Saturday evenings sell out first.

SUN 25 OCT · SINGAPORE

Little India, Orchard Road, river dinner

  • 09:30 Little India — Deepavali light-up along Serangoon Road; breakfast at Komala Vilas; Mustafa Centre
  • 12:30 Lunch at The Coconut Club, Beach Road — Bib Gourmand nasi lemak
  • 14:00 Orchard Road: ION, Takashimaya, Paragon
  • 17:30 Bumboat river cruise from Clarke Quay at dusk, 40 min
  • 19:00 Dinner: Jumbo Seafood chilli crab, or Peranakan at Candlenut (Michelin star)
Tip · Check Candlenut and Jumbo against the Dine & Save listing before booking.

MON 26 OCT · SINGAPORE → CHENNAI

Jewel Changi and home

  • Morning: pack; last shopping at Bugis Street
  • 14:00 Jewel Changi — Rain Vortex, Canopy Park nets and mazes, Shiseido Forest Valley
  • 17:00 Early dinner at Jewel: Song Fa Bak Kut Teh, Shake Shack, Burnt Ends Bakery
  • 18:00 Early check-in counters at Jewel where offered, else walk to the terminal
  • 19:30 Airside; 21:00 departure to Chennai
Tip · Rain Vortex light show runs in the evening — the 20:00 show is just before you head airside.
